    What is live and what is not

    Occupations, skills and the gap come from the U.S. Department of Labor's O*NET database (public domain, release 29.1), queried locally in your browser. Job listings are fetched live from Portland employers' public Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters and Ashby job boards. We match jobs to occupations on title keywords, which is rough — treat these as leads, not a filtered shortlist.

    Growth-sector figures are Oregon Employment Department projections for 2024–2034, published on QualityInfo.org. Those are projections: Portland metro employment is currently falling.
